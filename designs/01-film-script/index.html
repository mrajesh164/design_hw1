SCENE & SIP

a café in ten scenes

written for anyone who has ever
ordered a coffee like it was a line of dialogue

NAME ........ $PRICE — the drink and what it costs

(parenthetical) — what is actually in it

FADE IN:

INT. SCENE & SIP - MORNING

A narrow café with a low ceiling and a very good espresso machine. Eight hand-lettered signs hang over the counter, one for every kind of movie. A BARISTA wipes the bar with the calm of someone who has seen every ending.

BARISTA

(not looking up)

What kind of day are you having? I can work with any genre.

They slide a menu across the counter. The first page is dark.

INT. HORROR CORNER - NIGHT

HORROR MENU

The lights flicker. Something drips, probably the cold brew tap. A chalkboard reads: DO NOT GO IN THE BASEMENT.

THE FINAL GIRL$4.00

(dark roast, brewed black)

Hot enough to wake the dead. Survives to the sequel.

BASEMENT COLD BREW$5.00

(steeped for twenty-four hours)

Don't ask what else is down there.

THE CALL IS COMING FROM INSIDE$5.50

(blood orange shrub, sparkling)

Sweet, red, and a little too sharp.

CUT TO:

INT. SCI-FI BAR - SPACE

SCI-FI MENU

Cool blue light. The espresso machine hums at a frequency that doesn't seem entirely safe. Somewhere, a door hisses open.

WARP DRIVE$6.00

(espresso tonic with butterfly pea)

Starts blue. Ends purple. Nobody agrees on what happened in between.

FIRST CONTACT$6.50

(ceremonial matcha, edible glitter)

We come in peace. We also come in green.

REPLICANT$5.25

(oat flat white)

More human than the human kind.

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. NOIR LOUNGE - RAIN

NOIR MENU

Venetian blinds, one lamp. Rain on the window and a man in a coat who is clearly not here for the pastries.

BITTER END$5.00

(double espresso, amaro syrup, no sugar)

It's the kind of drink that knows your secrets.

THE LONG GOODBYE$4.50

(cortado, served slowly)

Takes about as long as the movie.

FEMME FATALE$4.75

(hibiscus iced tea)

Red, cold, and not what you expected.

DISSOLVE TO:

EXT. ROMANCE PATIO - GOLDEN HOUR

ROMANCE MENU

String lights. A table for two with only one chair pulled out, until someone arrives and fixes that. It begins to rain, and nobody minds.

MEET CUTE$8.00

(cappuccino and one croissant, two forks)

You both reached for the last one.

RAINSTORM KISS$5.75

(rose latte)

Soft, floral, a little dramatic.

THE GRAND GESTURE$6.25

(honey lavender latte, with a handwritten note)

Delivered at the airport. Please do not run.

MATCH CUT TO:

EXT. WESTERN HITCHING POST - HIGH NOON

WESTERN MENU

Dust. A wooden sign swings once in the wind. Two cups sit on the rail, ten paces apart.

HIGH NOON$3.75

(cowboy coffee, grounds and all)

Strong enough to settle a dispute.

THE GOOD, THE BAD, AND THE OATMEAL$7.00

(skillet oatmeal, three toppings)

Pick one. You know which one.

CUT TO:

INT. MYSTERY STUDY - NIGHT

MYSTERY MENU

Fog at the window. A single streetlamp, a magnifying glass on the desk, and a cup of tea that is still warm. Someone was just here.

THE USUAL SUSPECT$4.25

(Earl Grey with a lemon twist)

Hiding in plain sight.

RED HERRING$5.50

(smoked tea latte with a hint of cinnamon)

Not what it seems.

PLOT TWIST$6.00

(iced chai with a shot of espresso)

Sweet, then suddenly not.

DISSOLVE TO:

EXT. FANTASY CASTLE GATE - DAWN

FANTASY MENU

Emerald hills, gold light, a moon still hanging over the towers. Somewhere above the gate, something large shifts in its sleep.

THE CHOSEN ONE$6.50

(golden turmeric latte with honey)

Destined for greatness.

DRAGON'S BREATH$5.75

(spiced hot chocolate with chili and cinnamon)

Mind the fire.

ELIXIR OF LIFE$6.25

(sparkling elderflower lemonade with edible flowers)

Tastes like a spell that worked.

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. COMEDY CLUB - NIGHT

COMEDY MENU

Red curtains, a bright spotlight, and a microphone stand that nobody has fixed in years. Someone in the back row is already laughing.

SLAPSTICK$4.50

(banana milk latte)

Slips right down.

PUNCHLINE$5.25

(peanut butter mocha)

Sweet setup, salty finish.

LAUGH TRACK$5.00

(cold brew float with vanilla ice cream)

Canned laughter not included.

CUT TO:

INT. SCENE & SIP - LATER

The light has changed. The BARISTA flips the sign on the door. They look, for the first time, directly at the audience.

BARISTA

(softly)

We're open every day, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. No reservations. No sequels. Just come in.

The door opens. A bell rings. The scene begins again.

FADE OUT.

THE END
