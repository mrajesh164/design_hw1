HOME BREW

a café in five scenes

written for anyone who has ever
ordered a coffee like it was a line of dialogue

FINAL DRAFT — OPEN DAILY
1138 Marquee Lane
Hyde Park, Chicago
(555) 019-0420

FADE IN:

INT. HOME BREW - MORNING

A narrow café with a low ceiling and a very good espresso machine. Five hand-lettered signs hang over the counter, one for every kind of movie. A BARISTA wipes the bar with the calm of someone who has seen every ending.

BARISTA

(not looking up)

What kind of day are you having? I can work with any genre.

They slide a menu across the counter. The first page is dark.

INT. HORROR CORNER - NIGHT

The lights flicker. Something drips, probably the cold brew tap. A chalkboard reads: DO NOT GO IN THE BASEMENT.

THE FINAL GIRL

(dark roast, brewed black)

Hot enough to wake the dead. Survives to the sequel. $4.00

BASEMENT COLD BREW

(steeped for twenty-four hours)

Don't ask what else is down there. $5.00

THE CALL IS COMING FROM INSIDE

(blood orange shrub, sparkling)

Sweet, red, and a little too sharp. $5.50

CUT TO:

INT. SCI-FI BAR - SPACE

Cool blue light. The espresso machine hums at a frequency that doesn't seem entirely safe. Somewhere, a door hisses open.

WARP DRIVE

(espresso tonic with butterfly pea)

Starts blue. Ends purple. Nobody agrees on what happened in between. $6.00

FIRST CONTACT

(ceremonial matcha, edible glitter)

We come in peace. We also come in green. $6.50

REPLICANT

(oat flat white)

More human than the human kind. $5.25

SMASH CUT TO:

INT. NOIR LOUNGE - RAIN

Venetian blinds, one lamp. Rain on the window and a man in a coat who is clearly not here for the pastries.

BITTER END

(double espresso, amaro syrup, no sugar)

It's the kind of drink that knows your secrets. $5.00

THE LONG GOODBYE

(cortado, served slowly)

Takes about as long as the movie. $4.50

FEMME FATALE

(hibiscus iced tea)

Red, cold, and not what you expected. $4.75

DISSOLVE TO:

EXT. ROMANCE PATIO - GOLDEN HOUR

String lights. A table for two with only one chair pulled out, until someone arrives and fixes that. It begins to rain, and nobody minds.

MEET CUTE

(cappuccino and one croissant, two forks)

You both reached for the last one. $8.00

RAINSTORM KISS

(rose latte)

Soft, floral, a little dramatic. $5.75

THE GRAND GESTURE

(honey lavender latte, with a handwritten note)

Delivered at the airport. Please do not run. $6.25

MATCH CUT TO:

EXT. WESTERN HITCHING POST - HIGH NOON

Dust. A wooden sign swings once in the wind. Two cups sit on the rail, ten paces apart.

HIGH NOON

(cowboy coffee, grounds and all)

Strong enough to settle a dispute. $3.75

THE GOOD, THE BAD, AND THE OATMEAL

(skillet oatmeal, three toppings)

Pick one. You know which one. $7.00

INT. HOME BREW - LATER

The light has changed. The BARISTA flips the sign on the door. They look, for the first time, directly at the audience.

BARISTA

(softly)

We're open every day, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. No reservations. No sequels. Just come in.

The door opens. A bell rings. The scene begins again.

FADE OUT.

THE END
