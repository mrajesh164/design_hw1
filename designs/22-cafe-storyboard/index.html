A café where every drink is a genre.

Order a dark roast and you are in a horror movie. Ask for a rose latte and it turns into a romance. Our coffees, teas and seasonal drinks are sorted into eight film genres, so finding your favorite drink is like picking tonight’s movie.

About the café

Scene & Sip is a small neighborhood café with a big idea: every drink deserves a genre. Our baristas pull espresso, steep tea and shake up seasonal specials from early morning until late evening, and every one of them has a place on the menu below.

Pull up a stool, pick a genre and find a drink to match your mood. Come for the coffee, stay for the plot.

  • 8 film genres
  • 23 drinks
  • 7 to 10 open daily
